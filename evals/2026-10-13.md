## Finding and correction

Independent evaluation found ambiguity between “review where available” in the entrypoint and the required independent-review gate in the portability reference. The entrypoint now explicitly requires a fresh reviewer, fresh session or human review before a feature passes. A builder adopting a reviewer persona is still self-review.
